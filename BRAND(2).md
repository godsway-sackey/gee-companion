# Ahotɔ Wellness Enterprise — Brand Guide

**Version:** 1.0  
**Brand:** Ahotɔ Wellness  
**Formal business name:** Ahotɔ Wellness Enterprise  
**Primary tagline:** *Everyday Health. Everyday Wellness.*  
**Supporting line:** *Good Health Lives Here.*  

## 1. Brand idea

Ahotɔ Wellness is a community-focused health and wellness brand serving everyday people with basic health screening, medical consumables, health gadgets, and wellness products. The brand should feel **trusted, warm, accessible, clean, modern, and local** rather than overly clinical.

The customer-facing name is **Ahotɔ Wellness**. Use **Ahotɔ Wellness Enterprise** for formal documents, registrations, invoices, contracts, receipts, and other legal/business contexts.

## 2. Primary logo

The logo is intentionally simple and contains only three visual elements:

1. **Gold heart** — care, health, wellbeing, warmth.
2. **White ECG/pulse line** — screening, monitoring, vitality, health awareness.
3. **Forest-green dot** — the person/community at the centre of care.

The icon should not be combined with extra hands, leaves, crosses, shields, stethoscopes, or other symbols. The simplicity is part of the identity.

### Primary stacked lockup

- Icon centered above the wordmark.
- **Ahotɔ** in Ahotɔ Green.
- **WELLNESS** in Ahotɔ Gold, uppercase with generous tracking.
- Tagline may sit below in Ahotɔ Green when space permits.

### Horizontal lockup

Use the icon on the left, followed by the wordmark. The tagline can appear on the right or below for signage, web headers, receipts, letterheads, and wide-format applications.

### Icon-only mark

Use the gold heart + white pulse line + green dot where the full wordmark is too large, including:

- app icon or favicon
- social avatar
- profile image
- sticker seal
- small product label
- uniform embroidery

| Colour | Hex | RGB | Primary use |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **Ahotɔ Green** | `#176B57` | 23, 107, 87 | Wordmark, dot, headings, backgrounds, trust cues |
| **Ahotɔ Gold** | `#D9A441` | 217, 164, 65 | Heart, WELLNESS word, highlights, premium accents |
| **Ahotɔ Cream** | `#F7F3E8` | 247, 243, 232 | Warm backgrounds, print materials, packaging |
| **Charcoal** | `#26332F` | 38, 51, 47 | Body text, formal documents, high-legibility copy |
| **White** | `#FFFFFF` | 255, 255, 255 | ECG line, negative space, reverse layouts |

### Recommended colour balance

Use the palette with restraint:

- **60%** cream/white neutral space
- **25%** Ahotɔ Green
- **10%** Ahotɔ Gold
- **5%** Charcoal or supporting neutrals

Gold works best as an accent rather than a dominant page background.

## 4. Typography

The visual direction follows the type style used in the approved brand sheet.

### Primary family — Nunito Sans

Use **Nunito Sans** throughout the brand for its warm, rounded, highly readable character.

- **Nunito Sans ExtraBold / Bold** — Ahotɔ wordmark-style headings, key messages, signage headings.
- **Nunito Sans SemiBold** — subheadings, labels, navigation, form headings.
- **Nunito Sans Regular** — body copy, captions, forms, receipts, product information.

Suggested fallback stack:

```css
font-family: "Nunito Sans", Nunito, Arial, sans-serif;
```

### Accent handwriting — Caveat

Use a handwritten accent only for short human messages such as:

- *Good Health Lives Here.*
- *Healthier People. Stronger Communities.*

Use sparingly. Never use it for medical instructions, prices, legal text, product dosage, screening results, or long paragraphs.

Suggested fallback stack:

```css
font-family: "Caveat", "Segoe Print", cursive;
```

## 5. Tone of voice

Ahotɔ Wellness should sound:

- clear and reassuring
- friendly but professional
- simple and practical
- community-oriented
- health-conscious without sounding like a hospital

Avoid exaggerated medical claims, fear-based selling, miracle language, or promises of cure.

### Preferred language examples

- “Know your numbers. Live well.”
- “Everyday Health. Everyday Wellness.”
- “Good Health Lives Here.”
- “Simple checks. Smarter health choices.”
- “Health essentials for everyday living.”

## 6. Service descriptor

Use this standard descriptor where customers need to understand the business quickly:

**Health Screening • Medical Supplies • Health Gadgets • Wellness Products**

For smaller spaces, shorten to:

**Screening • Supplies • Gadgets • Wellness**

## 7. Logo spacing and size

Maintain clear space around the logo equal to at least the diameter of the green dot.

Recommended minimum sizes:

- Full stacked logo: **32 mm** wide in print / **140 px** digital.
- Horizontal logo: **45 mm** wide in print / **180 px** digital.
- Icon-only mark: **12 mm** print / **32 px** digital.

At very small sizes, omit the tagline.

## 8. Background rules

### Preferred

- white
- Ahotɔ Cream
- very light neutral backgrounds

### Dark background version

On Ahotɔ Green or dark charcoal:

- keep the gold heart
- keep the white ECG line
- keep the green dot only when sufficient contrast exists; otherwise use white for the dot in a dedicated reverse version
- use white wordmark text where necessary

## 9. Do not

Do not:

- add hands, leaves, crosses, shields, medical snakes, or extra wellness symbols to the approved icon
- change the heart to red
- recolour the ECG line away from white in the primary logo
- recolour the dot away from forest green in the primary logo
- stretch, skew, rotate, outline, bevel, emboss, or add strong shadows
- place the logo over a visually busy photograph without a clear panel
- use low-contrast versions
- replace `ɔ` with `o` in the brand name
- use several different handwritten fonts across materials

## 10. Core design system

### Corners

Use softly rounded corners for cards, labels, buttons, service tiles, and digital interfaces.

Suggested radius:

- small: 8 px
- medium: 16 px
- large: 24 px

### Lines and dividers

Use thin neutral or gold rules. Avoid heavy black borders.

### Iconography

Use simple outline icons with rounded ends. Prefer a single icon colour per group. Avoid mixing several icon families on one design.

### Photography

When photography is used, favour:

- real people and community health contexts
- warm natural light
- clean shelves/products
- health screening interactions
- home health monitoring

Avoid intimidating hospital imagery unless the context specifically requires it.

## 12. Suggested layout principles

Ahotɔ designs should use:

- generous white/cream space
- one clear message per design
- strong green headings
- gold for emphasis
- no more than 2 type families
- restrained icon use
- simple 2–4 column grids
- high legibility on mobile screens
